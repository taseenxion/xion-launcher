## Xion+ is on your account now

No more license key and no more Device ID. Xion+ is a normal purchase on your **Xion account**, so it unlocks everywhere you sign in: the launcher, the shop and Xion Client.

### What changed

- **Sign in and it just works.** Buy Xion+ on the shop while signed in, the seller approves the payment, and Xion+ appears on that account. Sign in with the same email in Xion Launcher and everything is unlocked — in the launcher and in game.
- **No key to paste.** The Xion+ card now shows your account status instead of a key box and a Device ID. If an approval lands while the launcher is open, it refreshes in the background (or press *Sync now*).
- **Follows you between PCs.** Because Xion+ lives on the account, a new laptop only needs a sign-in. It is no longer tied to one computer.
- **Capes you bought individually** stay unlocked too, even without the full Xion+ package.
- **Old keys keep working.** If you bought before accounts, your existing `XION-` key still verifies and unlocks everything exactly as before. The card offers to remove it once you have moved to an account.

### In the shop

- The **Buy Xion+** button now adds Xion+ to your cart (one-time $5 · ৳150 · ₹350) instead of opening the old key form.
- Checkout asks you to sign in first, so the approval is attached to the right account.
- Your order page shows Xion+ unlocking on the account, with the same pending → approved flow as cosmetics.

### In game

- Xion Client reads the account entitlement handed over by the launcher. Xion+ capes, Turbo, premium themes, chroma, custom crosshairs, pro HUD modules and the gold badge all unlock together.
- Individually purchased capes unlock on their own.
- The gold badge still appears for other Xion Client players.

### Also fixed

- The release script now copes with the Vercel project's Root Directory setting, so website deploys no longer fail with `The specified Root Directory "website" does not exist`.
- Store pages keep their download links in sync with the release.
