# Xion Launcher 2.2.0 — Event Horizon, in game

## The new look comes to Xion Client

Everything you see in game now matches the launcher.

- **The black hole is in game too.** The main menu and every menu backdrop show the same black hole as the launcher, turning slowly, coloured by your theme, with a gentle drift that follows your mouse.
- **A calmer main menu.** Your name with a line that draws itself in beneath it, a "Ready to play" status, an ivory Singleplayer button, and a clean Quick join list.
- **A cleaner Xion menu (Right Shift).** One black panel framed by fine lines, a quiet light that travels along its top edge, a thin accent mark on the open tab, and ivory switches. The HUD, Features, FPS, Capes, XION AI and Profile tabs all share it.
- **The Mods library** uses the same cards, filters and switches, with no more navy and purple.
- **Vanilla screens** (Options, world and server lists) use matching buttons, sliders and text fields.
- **Smoother motion.** Buttons ease in and out of hover evenly at any frame rate, and links underline themselves as you point at them.

## Unchanged

Everything from **2.1.0** remains: the black-hole launcher, 19 HD Originals capes, Xion+ on your account, the shop, XION AI and auto-update. Your settings, HUD layout and mods are untouched.

All six client versions (1.21.1, 1.21.4, 1.21.8, 1.21.10, 1.21.11 and 26.3) were rebuilt. Windows installer/portable and Linux AppImage/DEB/tar.gz update files are attached to this release.
