# Xion Launcher 2.0.0 — Nineteen HD Originals

## Eleven new HD capes

The **HD Originals** collection grows from eight to nineteen creator-made capes. Every new cape keeps the artist's lossless 1024×512 texture, its original frame timing (up to **48 frames**) and a matching elytra:

- **Blue Ruin** — branching blue lightning crackling out from a glowing core.
- **Changing Moon** — a lone tree on still water while the moon shifts behind it.
- **Crimson Dawn** — a red sun over a snowy peak with drifting cherry blossoms.
- **Four-Leaf Clover** — a glowing lucky clover under twinkling stars.
- **Gothic Altar** — a cathedral window, a cross and flickering candles.
- **Gothic Cross** — a radiant cross shining through a field of stars.
- **Heart Card** — a glowing ace of hearts with sparkles and floating hearts.
- **Misty Peaks** — moonlit mountains and pine forests wrapped in mist.
- **Serpent Blade** — a serpent coiled around a dark blade.
- **Spirit Stag** — a glowing spirit stag in a moonlit forest.
- **Thorn Crosses** — three swords bound in thorns.

They are included with **Xion+**, or can be bought one by one in the shop. Pick them in the launcher's cape locker or the in-game Xion menu; other Xion Client players see them too.

## Fixes

- Changing a setting (RAM, resolution, Java path…) no longer signs you out of your Xion account, undoes a cape you just picked or removes a licence key you just activated.
- Other players' Xion badges show up in game again: the presence service was rebuilt on the new storage.

## Unchanged

Everything from **1.27.1** remains: the brush-X mark and animated name badges, Xion+ on your account, the shop, XION AI, the FPS/VSync fix and auto-update.

Windows installer/portable and Linux AppImage/DEB/tar.gz update files are attached to this release.
