## Accounts, everywhere

Xion now has real accounts: one email and password for **the shop, the website and the launcher**. Sign in once and your cosmetics follow you — bought on the web, approved by the seller, worn in game.

### What you can do

- **Create an account** with any email (Gmail works) and your Minecraft name, on the shop or in the launcher. One account per Minecraft name.
- **Buy signed in.** Checkout fills in your name and email for you, and the order is attached to the account instead of this browser.
- **Everything in one locker.** The shop's Locker section, the website's account panel and the launcher's Settings → Xion account all show the same owned capes, hats, wings and auras.
- **Wear it from anywhere.** Equip a cape on the web and the launcher picks it up: it is applied on your next launch, and other Xion Client players see it immediately.
- **Orders that follow you.** Your order history and keys are on the account, so a new laptop is not a new story.

### In the launcher

Settings now has a **Xion account** card: sign in or create an account, see what you own, click a cape you own to wear it, and press *Sync now* after a purchase is approved. The account is remembered between launches, and it quietly syncs in the background when the launcher starts. Purchased capes stay unlocked in the cape locker even without a Xion+ license key.

### Also

- Signed-out players lose nothing: the locker-key flow from 1.23 still works, and orders placed as a guest stay on that browser.
- Logging out clears the account from this computer; your purchases stay on the account.

Nothing else changed in this build — it is the 1.23.1 client with accounts added.
