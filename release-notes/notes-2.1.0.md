# Xion Launcher 2.1.0 — Event Horizon

## A new launcher

Xion Launcher has a completely new look.

- **A live black hole** behind everything. It is rendered in real time: starlight bends around the horizon, the accretion disk turns slowly, the side spinning toward you glows brighter, and a thin photon ring traces the shadow. On Home it hangs above your name; on other pages it drifts back and dims behind your work.
- **Your theme colours the disk.** Nightfall, Aurora, Glacier, Sakura, Ember and Void each light the black hole in their own colours, and the change glides in. In game, themes still set the menus, colours and mountain backdrop.
- **A quieter, sharper interface.** Names and titles in a classic serif, small labels in mono, thin lines instead of rounded bubbles, and one accent colour used sparingly.
- **News and Quick join** are clean lists you can scan at a glance.

## Lighter on your PC

The black hole adjusts its own resolution to keep the launcher smooth, draws less often on other pages, slows to a trickle while Minecraft is running and stops when the window is hidden. The old mountain images are gone from the launcher, so the download is a little smaller.

## Unchanged

Everything from **2.0.0** remains: 19 HD Originals capes, Xion+ on your account, the shop, XION AI, animated badges and auto-update.

Windows installer/portable and Linux AppImage/DEB/tar.gz update files are attached to this release.
