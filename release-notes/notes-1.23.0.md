# Xion Launcher 1.23.0

## 12 new animated capes

A new **Anime** set in the cape locker, painted frame by frame (8 frames each):

| Cape | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Sunrise Torii | A torii gate on the water at first light, birds crossing a burning sky |
| Sakura Dusk | Cherry boughs over a violet dusk, petals drifting the whole time |
| Autumn Fall | A dark wood in October with leaves coming down for good |
| Neon Rain | A back street after midnight, rain that never stops |
| Moonlit Wolf | A wolf on the ridge under a huge moon, mist crawling past |
| Moonlit Bamboo | Bamboo stalks, deep green dark, fireflies that wander |
| Starfall | Midnight blue with meteors cutting across |
| Ember Dusk | Burnt red sky, embers going up instead of down |
| Moon Tide | Cold sea at night, wave crests rolling under a moon |
| Snow Lantern | A paper lantern in the snow, warm light against slate |
| Shadow Eyes | Almost nothing, then two eyes open in the dark. Blinks |
| Night Garden | Indigo garden, glowing flowers, fireflies between them |

Three of them are free for everyone (Sunrise Torii, Moonlit Bamboo, Snow Lantern),
the rest are part of Xion+.

## More FPS: the OG preset

Settings → Performance has a fourth mode. **OG** is the old-client extreme on top
of Turbo: 6 chunk render distance, 5 simulation distance, no chunk fade-in, no
weather radius, half entity distance, 260 fps cap, Sodium at its fastest and a
garbage collector tuned for 20 ms pauses. Free for everyone.

## More OG toggles in game

The **FPS** tab (Right Shift → FPS) gained four more switches, all applied the
moment you click them:

- Render distance 6
- Simulation distance 5
- Unlimited FPS (cap 260)
- No chunk fade-in (1.21.11 and newer)

## Four more mods in the pack

C2ME, ThreadTweak, Memory Leak Fix and FastLoad join the performance pack: chunk
loading on every core, higher thread priority, no memory creep on long sessions,
and world loading without stalls.

## Mods page cleaned up

- The list is split into **XION MODS** and **YOUR MODS** with counts, and the
  filter chips only react while they are actually visible.
- Empty sections say so instead of showing a blank grid, and status pills no
  longer overflow their card.

## Admin on the website

**xionx.vercel.app/admin** now has three tabs behind the same login: Xion+
orders, cape orders (approve, reject, delete) and users (grant or revoke a
cosmetic, reset an account key).

## Also

- Windows installer and portable exe, Linux AppImage, .deb and tar.gz, published
