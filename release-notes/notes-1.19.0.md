- The Xion Client menu is now built from the current source: press Right Shift in game and it opens straight on Mods
  - Every mod in the profile as its own card, sliding in one after another, logo popping in after the card lands
  - Logos are transparent: each mod's icon has its flat background cut away, trimmed and centred, so nothing sits in a box
  - Switch any mod on or off with its switch, or open that mod's own settings with the ⚙ button
  - Filter by Xion Pack or the mods you added yourself; mods other mods depend on stay safely on
  - Changes apply the next time you press Play
- Mods you switch off stay off: the Xion Pack no longer downloads them again on the next launch
- Fixed: the in-game client in 1.18.x was built from an older source tree, so some players saw no Mods page at all
