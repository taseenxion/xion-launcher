## In the client and shop

- **144 capes** in the synchronized catalog; **New HD** highlights this collection in the in-game menu and shop.
- HD textures decode on a background worker. Lightweight client thumbnails avoid loading every HD animation into GPU memory, and unused worn HD textures are released when switching capes.
- Other players' capes use the correct animation timing and a bounded texture cache; bundled textures can be read locally instead of repeatedly downloaded.
- The launcher and web previews keep the original frame timings and resolution, including matching elytra views.
- Your original source sheets and GIF previews remain untouched.

## A simpler Xion logo

The client and matching app/web branding now use a minimal, clean **X** with restrained moonlight colour. No extra scenery, dot or shield in the brand mark. The animated free/premium name badges remain separate.

All six client targets were rebuilt, with Windows installer/portable and Linux AppImage/DEB/tar.gz update assets. The FPS fix, animated badges, XION AI and account/cape fixes from earlier releases remain included.
